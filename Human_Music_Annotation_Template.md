# Human Music Annotation Sheet & Workflow Template

This template is designed for professional human music annotators to log track structures, instrumentation details, and global musical features. 

Ensure that no parentheses are used anywhere in the entire completed document. All lists under Instruments must have every item end with a comma, including the last item.

---

## Part 1: Track Logging Procedure

Follow this direct chronological sequence to analyze the track:

1. **Precision Listening:** Listen to the track starting from 00:00. Mark the start time of each new section precisely to the second.
2. **Boundary Detection:** Identify section boundaries by listening for key structural indicators:
   - Entrance or exit of key percussion, bass lines, or vocal styles.
   - Significant dynamic shifts or chord changes.
3. **No Overlapping/Large Gaps:** Ensure that the end of one section aligns perfectly with the start of the next.
4. **Length Restriction:** Split any section exceeding 50 seconds into smaller, logical segments using subtle internal changes like a drum fill or light instrumentation addition. No single segment should exceed 60 seconds.
5. **Standalone Descriptions:** Write standalone narrative descriptions for each segment without referencing previous or future sections.

---

## Part 2: Timeline Section Template

Copy and paste this blank block for each structural segment you identify in the audio track.

```markdown
- **Start Time:** MM:SS
- **Section Type:** Select from: intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, post-chorus, bridge, breakdown, build-up, drop, outro, solo / instrumental, interlude, transition, vamp
- **Narrative Description:** Write exactly 1 to 2 factual, objective sentences about the active musical elements in this specific segment, strictly following the formulas/templates from the BP MASTER MUSIC ANNOTATION WRITING STYLE GUIDE for the section type. Do not analyze lyrics. Do not refer to other segments. Keep this completely standalone. Avoid parentheses.
- **Instruments:** List every single instrument present in this segment, separated by commas. Every instrument name must end with a comma, including the last one. Example: Drums, Electric Bass, Synth Pad,
- **Vibe Tags:** List comma-separated mood or energy descriptors from the taxonomy.
- **Melody:** Melody Field Analysis (High Priority): Provide a precise, careful, and deeply analytical breakdown of the melody, limited to a maximum of 1 sentence. Avoid generic, indiscriminate observations (e.g., instead of just saying "the melody is catchy/simple," analyze its contour, phrasing, movement, and musical value). Show how the melody functions, how it interacts with other layers, and how it drives the track's specific mood. Previous restrictions on melody form are removed.
```

## Part 3: Global Musical Matrix Template

After completing the timeline log, fill out this global summary of the entire song.

```markdown
Global Musical Matrix
1. Genre, Era & Scene: Write 1 to 2 complete sentences explaining and showing the track's genre, subgenre, and historical or aesthetic context. Be precise and avoid excessive labels.
2. Tempo, Rhythm & Groove: Write 1 to 2 sentences describing the tempo, specific drum patterns, time signature, and the leading rhythmic instrument.
3. Harmony, Chords & Key: Write 1 to 2 sentences describing the tonal feeling, chord qualities, and harmonic color. Stick to descriptive results and do not state the exact key or chord progression as you may get confused.
4. Instrumentation: Identify the dominant and supporting instruments across the entire track in 1 to 2 sentences.
5. Playing Style: Detail the performance techniques, articulation, and physical energy of the performers in 1 to 2 sentences.
6. Wow Factor: Describe the most unique, memorable, or technically impressive aspect of the track in 1 to 2 sentences.
7. Emotion, Vibe & Setting: Outline the overall emotional tone, energy level, and ideal listening setting in 1 to 2 sentences.
8. Mix & Production: Describe the mixing choices, spatial reverb, panning, compression, and master dynamics in 1 to 2 sentences.
9. Sonic Fidelity: Factual description of the recording quality, noise floor, and warmth characteristics in 1 to 2 sentences.
10. Vocal Expression: Factual description of vocal delivery, registration, harmonies, or style in 1 to 2 sentences. Write N/A if instrumental.
11. Lyrical Evocation: General overview of lyrical themes and narrative themes in 1 to 2 sentences. Write N/A if instrumental.
12. Genre / Era / Scene (Tag List): Pure words only, comma-separated. Do not write sentences.
13. Dominant Instruments (Tag List): Pure words only, comma-separated. Do not write sentences. Every instrument must end with a comma, including the last one.

Song title: Write the track title here

Song by: Write the artist name here

Performing Artists: Write the performing bands or session musicians here

Lyrics & Song Structure: Copy and paste the lyric sheet or structural breakdown here
```
